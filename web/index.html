FDI Two-Digit Dental Numbering Standard:

The World Dental Federation (FDI) uses a systematic two-digit designation for permanent teeth:

🔢 1st Digit: Jaw Quadrant
  • Quadrant 1 (10s): Upper Right — Teeth #11 to #18
  • Quadrant 2 (20s): Upper Left — Teeth #21 to #28
  • Quadrant 3 (30s): Lower Left — Teeth #31 to #38
  • Quadrant 4 (40s): Lower Right — Teeth #41 to #48
🦷 2nd Digit: Tooth Position (Anterior to Posterior)
  • 1 & 2: Central and Lateral Incisors — Single rooted
  • 3: Canine (Cuspid) — Longest single root in jaw
  • 4 & 5: 1st and 2nd Premolars (Bicuspids) — 1 or 2 roots
  • 6, 7 & 8: 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Molars — Multi-rooted (2 to 3 roots)
